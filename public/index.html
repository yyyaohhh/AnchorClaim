Capabilities

An end-to-end claim engine, not a spreadsheet.

Every dollar is traced from the contract clause to the settlement transaction — fraud-checked and settled automatically, with no manual step.

AI charter-party parsing

Extracts laytime, daily rate and suspension clauses from free-text contracts into structured data — with a confidence score on every field.

AIS cross-check

Compares the port's Statement of Facts against actual AIS in-port hours, flagging any material delta as a potential fraud before money moves.

Automatic demurrage calc

Deducts contractual suspension time — weather, strikes, congestion — and computes the exact penalty to the dollar, pro-rata by the day.

On-chain escrow settlement

Writes an evidence hash and the amount to an escrow contract that deducts the penalty, pays the owner, and refunds the remainder — immutably.

Zero-touch settlement

Signed, verified voyages settle automatically — the agent deducts the penalty, pays the owner and refunds the remainder with no manual step.

Explainable output

Every figure expands into a step-by-step breakdown — gross hours → exclusions → counted → excess → rate → total — so auditors can verify it.

How it works

Five stages, one verifiable flow.

Watch each stage execute live in the demo, from raw contract text to an on-chain attestation.

01

Parse charter party

LLM extraction of laytime, rate and exclusions into structured JSON.

02

Gather multi-source evidence

AIS, weather, satellite, port community, NOR and news — six independent sources, AIS included.

03

AI agents vote

Three independent agents weigh all six sources and decide what's corroborated.

04

Compute demurrage

Deduct suspensions and calculate the exact penalty owed.

05

Settle on-chain

Submit evidence hash + amount to escrow for settlement.

Why on-chain

Built for trust between charterers and owners.

Settlement is deterministic, signed by evidence, and auditable by both parties.

No manual reconciliation

The agent replaces weeks of back-and-forth with a repeatable, second-scale computation.

Fraud-resistant by design

SoF vs AIS cross-checks verify every port log before funds move, so falsified logs can't inflate a settlement.

Fully automated signing

Every result is signed by evidence and settled on-chain in seconds — no queues, no manual approvers.

On-chain evidence

One hash. Full audit trail.

The contract, port log and AIS track are folded into a single SHA-256 evidence hash, written to the escrow contract alongside the amount.
